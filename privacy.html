Typez

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Typez is designed to make typing more useful without turning what you type into a product. This policy explains what the Typez app and this website handle, where information is kept, and the choices available to you.

1. Keyboard input and surrounding text

Typez does not retain, upload, sell, or use the text you type for advertising. Keystrokes are sent to the text field in the app you are using.

To provide cursor navigation, suggestions, capitalization, and punctuation, the keyboard may process the limited surrounding text that iOS makes available to keyboard extensions. This processing happens on your device. Typez does not retain or upload that surrounding text.

2. Optional app analytics

If you choose “Share App Analytics,” Typez uses Amplitude, Inc. to understand how the containing app is used. Typez records app opens, screens viewed, onboarding steps completed, paywall views, and paywall actions. Event timestamps are recorded automatically.

Events include a randomly generated installation identifier and limited technical information such as the app version, operating-system name, and operating-system version. Typez does not use analytics for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Analytics never includes keys you press, words you type, surrounding text, clipboard contents, saved clipboard items, contact information, or the identity of apps where the keyboard is used. The Amplitude SDK is linked only to the Typez containing app; it is not linked to or run by the keyboard extension.

Typez disables Amplitude autocapture, session replay, IDFV, IP-address event properties, location properties, carrier information, language properties, and device-model properties. Analytics is off until you make a choice. You can turn “Share App Analytics” on or off at any time in Typez Settings. Turning it off stops future analytics collection from the app.

3. Information stored on your device

Typez may store the following information locally:

  • Clipboard items you explicitly save or import
  • Pinned status and ordering for saved clipboard items
  • Recently used emoji
  • Keyboard preferences such as theme, font, rows, and haptics
  • Keyboard setup and Typez Pro entitlement status

This information is stored in Typez's local app containers and, when needed to share it between the containing app and keyboard, its iOS App Group container. Removing the app generally removes its locally stored data, subject to normal iOS behavior and any device backups you control.

4. Full Access and clipboard use

Core typing and cursor navigation work without Full Access. Some optional functionality, including making saved clipboard items available inside the keyboard, requires Full Access because iOS otherwise prevents the keyboard and containing app from sharing their stored information.

Typez reads text from the system clipboard only after you explicitly choose an import action. While the clipboard panel is open, Typez may check whether the clipboard contains text and whether it has changed; this metadata check does not read the clipboard text. Enabling Full Access does not cause Typez to upload your typing or document text.

5. Purchases

If Typez offers purchases, Apple processes the transaction. Typez does not receive your full payment-card details. The app may store the entitlement needed to remember whether Pro features are available to you.

6. This website

The current website does not ask for an account, email address, or other personal information, and it does not use advertising trackers or third-party behavioral analytics.

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like other hosting providers, Vercel may process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, and request logs to deliver and secure the website under its own terms and privacy practices.

7. Sharing and service providers

Typez does not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers only when necessary to provide Typez: Amplitude processes optional app analytics when you enable it, Vercel hosts this website, and Apple distributes the app and processes purchases. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Amplitude processes analytics on our behalf under its Privacy Notice.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

On-device information remains until you delete it in Typez, reset the relevant settings, or remove the app. Typez does not currently maintain user accounts or a remote copy of your saved keyboard data.

You can disable or remove the Typez keyboard at any time in iOS Settings. You can also turn off Full Access without losing core typing and cursor navigation.

Optional analytics is retained only as long as needed to understand product usage and improve Typez, with a target maximum retention period of 12 months. Turning off “Share App Analytics” stops future collection. You may also contact the developer to request deletion of analytics associated with your installation.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Typez changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Material changes will be communicated when appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or analytics deletion requests, use Contact Support in Typez Settings or the support contact listed on the Typez App Store page. For website feedback that does not contain personal information, you may use the Typez website repository.
