How to Move the Cursor Precisely on iPhone

Fix a typo in the middle of a word without deleting everything after it.

By Javokhir, creator of Typez · Published November 25, 2026
Published by Typez, an iPhone keyboard app.

Touch and hold the spacebar on Apple’s iPhone keyboard until the keys go blank. Drag to move the cursor, then release. You can also drag the insertion point directly in the text. If you prefer arrow keys, Typez lets you move one character or one word at a time.

Move the cursor with the spacebar

Trying to tap between two letters often places the cursor at the wrong end of a word. The built-in trackpad is usually a better starting point.

  1. Tap the text you want to edit so the keyboard appears.
  2. Press and hold the spacebar until the key labels disappear.
  3. Without lifting your finger, slide it in the direction you want the cursor to move.
  4. Release when the cursor reaches the correct position.

Try it in Notes with a long sentence. Move slowly when you are close to the letter you want to correct.

Place the cursor between two letters

You can also touch and hold the insertion point in editable text, then drag it to the correct spot. A magnified view can help you see the letters while your finger is over them.

For example, if you typed recieve, position the cursor near the incorrect letters and make the correction. If the entire word is wrong, double-tap it and replace it instead.

Use arrow keys for precise cursor movement

Apple’s standard iPhone letter keyboard does not have a dedicated row of cursor arrow keys. Typez takes a different approach: you can move left or right by one character, move by whole words, or hold a control to keep moving.

Character controls are useful for punctuation, typos, URLs and codes. Word controls help you move across a longer sentence without dragging the cursor the entire distance.

Try these three everyday edits

  • Missing punctuation: change 630 to 6:30 by inserting the colon in the middle.
  • Wrong character in a link: move to the specific character instead of deleting the rest of the URL.
  • Wrong date: navigate to the date and replace only the part that needs changing.

For isolated edits, the spacebar gesture may be all you need. If you make small corrections repeatedly, dedicated cursor buttons are worth trying.

Why is the iPhone cursor difficult to move?

If direct tapping keeps selecting a word or moving to its edge, try the spacebar gesture instead. If a third-party keyboard is missing, test it in Notes; password fields and some other fields require Apple’s keyboard.
