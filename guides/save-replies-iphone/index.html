How to Save Frequently Used Text and Phrases on iPhone

Stop typing the same addresses, links and replies over and over.

By Javokhir, creator of Typez · Published November 27, 2026
Published by Typez, an iPhone keyboard app.

Use iPhone Text Replacement for short phrases you can trigger with a shortcut. For longer templates, keep them in Notes. If you want to browse and insert saved text directly from the keyboard, use a saved-text keyboard such as Typez.

What text should you save?

Start with text you type or paste several times a week: your email address, business address, appointment link, a standard reply, or a message template.

Keep the reusable part separate from names, dates and other details that change. Do not store passwords, verification codes or private customer information in general-purpose templates.

Use iPhone Text Replacement for quick shortcuts

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement.
  2. Tap +.
  3. Enter the text you want to reuse in Phrase.
  4. Enter an abbreviation in Shortcut and tap Save.
  5. Try the abbreviation with Apple’s keyboard in Notes.

For example, use zzmail for your email address or zzbook for an appointment link. A distinctive prefix helps prevent accidental expansion while typing ordinary words.

Examples of reusable phrases and replies

  • Email: your business email address.
  • Booking: “You can choose a time here: [link].”
  • Support: “Could you send your reference number and a short description of the problem?”
  • Availability: “We’re available [days] from [hours].”
  • Address: your office or delivery address.

Replace placeholders and read the message before sending it.

Can you save multiple copied texts on iPhone?

Apple’s normal Paste action uses the current clipboard item, not a browsable history of previous copies. If you want to keep multiple texts for later, save each one deliberately using Notes or a clipboard keyboard.
